Simone de Beauvoir

Existentialist philosopher and feminist pioneer who examined freedom, ethical responsibility, and the social construction of gender.

Era: 20th century • Main themes: Freedom, Ethics, Gender

Introduction

De Beauvoir combined existentialist philosophy with political engagement, arguing that the oppressed must seize freedom for themselves and others.

Key Ideas

She argued that gender is a lived experience shaped by social norms, not a fixed biological destiny.

Legacy

Her work remains foundational for feminist theory and for contemporary debates about identity and autonomy.

Notable Quotes

“I am too intelligent, too demanding, and too resourceful for anyone to be able to take charge of me entirely.”

“Change your life today. Don't gamble on the future, act now, without delay.”
